# 📊 Executive Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard (FY2023)

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project provides an end-to-end business intelligence solution in Microsoft Excel to analyze annual sales performance, regional profitability, and sales representative KPIs. The dashboard bridges raw transactional data and strategic decision-making for executive stakeholders.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem & Objectives
- **Identify Revenue Drivers**: Evaluate total revenue and net profit across product lines and regions.
- **Performance Benchmarking**: Analyze individual sales representative efficiency and margin contribution.
- **Operational Optimization**: Provide dynamic summary metrics to detect underperforming territories and profit leakages.

---

## 🛠️ Key Excel Skills & Features Demonstrated
- **Data Architecture & Hygiene**: Separated raw inputs (`Cleaned_Data`) from reporting interfaces (`Executive_Dashboard`).
- **Dynamic Formulas & Logic**: Leveraged `SUMIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `XLOOKUP`, and calculated columns for automated aggregations.
- **KPI Metrics & Layout**: Designed minimalist KPI Cards for instant C-Suite metric reading.
- **Financial Formatting**: Clean number formatting (`$#,##0`, `0.0%`), hidden gridlines, and dark executive color palette (`#1B365D`).

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Recommendations
1. **Top Regional Market**: The **North & West Regions** contributed over 58% of total revenue with consistent high-margin product adoption.
2. **Product Profitability**: Enterprise Suite yielded the highest net profit per transaction, while entry-level Hardware Nodes required high volume to sustain margins.
3. **Representative Efficiency**: Top sales reps achieved an average profit margin above **28.5%**, showing opportunity for standardizing high-value sales playbooks across the team.
